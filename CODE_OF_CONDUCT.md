# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a respectful, harassment-free project for everyone,
regardless of background, identity, experience, or level of technical ability.

## Expected behavior

- Be considerate, specific, and constructive.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Respect privacy and do not post another person's private data or content.
- Accept maintainer decisions made to protect users, contributors, or legal and
  security boundaries.
- Avoid sexualized, threatening, discriminatory, or deliberately disruptive behavior.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and may temporarily or permanently
restrict participation when this policy is violated. For a private report, use
the reporting route in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) and prefix the report title
with `Conduct`. Do not publish sensitive evidence in an issue.

Enforcement decisions should document the applicable rule, protect reporters'
privacy, and avoid conflicts of interest. The project will establish an
independent escalation contact as the maintainer team grows.
